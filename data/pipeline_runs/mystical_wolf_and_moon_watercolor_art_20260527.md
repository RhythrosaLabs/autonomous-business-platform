## Product Description

There are moments when the moon pulls something loose inside you — something wild, something ancient, something that has always known the forest better than the city. This art print captures that feeling exactly: a lone wolf dissolving into watercolor mist beneath a luminous full moon, painted in deep indigo and rose-gold, as if the wilderness itself bled onto paper. Hang it where you need to remember that you are, at your core, untamed.

*Mystical Wolf and Moon* is a fine-art watercolor print produced at museum quality on heavyweight 230gsm matte paper. The design features hand-painted style brushwork with authentic pigment blooms and deliberate ink splashes — detail that catches the eye differently every time the light changes. Available in three sizes (8×10, 11×14, and 18×24), it arrives with a 1-inch white border ready for standard frames. Colours are rendered with archival-grade inks that resist fading for decades.

This piece was made for the wolf-hearted: the person whose walls tell a story about connection to nature, to spirit, to something bigger than a news feed. It gifts perfectly for birthdays, housewarmings, or anyone going through a season of transformation. Whether it lives above a writing desk, in a meditation corner, or in a bedroom gallery wall, it brings a quiet, powerful energy to any space.
